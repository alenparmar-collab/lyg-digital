# Voice and humour

## Who we sound like
A slightly older friend from youth group who is funny, kind, and takes the faith seriously without taking themselves seriously. Warm, direct, a little dry. Sentence case everywhere. Short sentences, but not all the same length.

## Where humour lives
- Margin notes
- Sticker labels and "Other" options
- Loading lines
- Empty states and the success screen
- The odd subtitle

## Where humour never goes
- Scripture, prayer, Mass, the Eucharist, Mary, the saints' holiness, anyone's faith or doubt
- Consent, safeguarding, privacy and guardian details
- Error messages that stop someone finishing
- Anything during the Triduum

The rule of thumb: joke about youth group life (late starts, chairs, samosas, the WhatsApp group, the one person who always brings a guitar), never about God.

## Examples that fit
- Margin note beside "Carrying chairs": "An underrated ministry."
- Margin note on the interests page: "Pick as many as you like. We won't hold you to all of them."
- Under "Patron saint (optional)": "Don't have one yet? We'll introduce you."
- Loading: "Asking St. Anthony to find your details..."
- Empty "Other" field placeholder: "Competitive chai making, maybe?"
- Success line: "You're in. Save a seat for someone next Sunday."
- Returning member: "Welcome back. Let's see what's changed."
- Small print chapter title: "The small print (it's actually important)."

## Examples that don't fit
- "Get ready to be on fire for God!!!" (performed hype, cliché)
- "Join the movement." (brand-speak)
- "Jesus is your homie." (flippant)
- "Unlock exclusive member benefits." (SaaS)
- Emoji in headings or buttons.

## Words to avoid in UI copy
submit, user, form, field, required*, invalid, profile, account, dashboard, journey, unlock, exclusive, awesome, amazing, blessed (as a hashtag word).
Prefer: send, you, page, chapter, needed, "that doesn't look like a phone number", your card, your details.

## Error copy
Plain and specific, no jokes.
- "We need your first name."
- "That doesn't look like a 10-digit mobile number."
- "Please ask a parent or guardian to add their details. We need this for anyone under 18."

## Faith language
Speak about faith the way you would to a friend after Mass. Name Christ plainly when it matters ("We exist because Christ called us friends.") and otherwise let the structure do the work. Avoid insider jargon unless it is explained.
